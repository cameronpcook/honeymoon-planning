🇰🇷 Seoul

Modern, fast-paced, food-and-nightlife-driven — palaces, K-beauty spas, street food markets, a great cocktail scene. Leans a bit more toward a 24-year-old's honeymoon energy unless you deliberately slow it down. 8 days on the ground, about 9 door-to-door.

Season

Shoulder: September–October (mild, early fall color).

Peak: July–August (hot, humid, monsoon risk in July).

Alaska's Seattle–Seoul route (operated by Hawaiian Airlines) isn't daily — outbound runs about 5x/week — so confirm the exact schedule against your dates before booking.

Budget for $7,500
$$$$$

Two splurge nights at the Four Seasons plus the rest at a stylish boutique like Hotel28 keeps lodging under $2,500 for the week, and Korean food and transit are inexpensive too — genuinely one of the better values on this list, even with a real splurge built in.
