🏝️ Bora Bora

The most iconic overwater-bungalow destination on Earth — Mount Otemanu backdrop, turquoise lagoon, extremely quiet and private. The purest “do nothing, look at water” honeymoon on this list.

Over budget at 7 nights

Even 5–6 nights at these properties can exceed $7,500 on lodging alone before food (also expensive, since almost everything is imported). If Bora Bora is the dream, consider extending the budget or planning 4–5 nights instead of 7.
