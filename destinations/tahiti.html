🌺 Papeete / Moorea

The classic honeymoon fantasy — lagoons, overwater bungalows, total quiet. Tahiti/Papeete is mostly a gateway; most of your time will be on Moorea for the postcard experience. 7 days on the ground, about 8 door-to-door.

Season

Shoulder: September–October (dry season winding down, fewer visitors).

Peak: July–August (dry season peak, most crowded/priciest for French Polynesia overall).

Air Tahiti Nui ended its Seattle–Papeete nonstop in early 2026 — this now routes through LAX, which flies 7–11x/week, so there's no tight seasonal window tied to the flight itself.

Budget for $7,500
$$$$$

Six nights in an overwater bungalow (Hilton or Sofitel, ~$650/night) runs about $3,900 before French Polynesia's ~13% TVA and resort fees, which push it closer to $4,800. Food is genuinely expensive on a resort island, and a couple of excursions add more — the total lands right around $7,500. Tight, but doable if you skip extra add-ons.

Itinerary
01

Arrive Papeete

Quick ferry to Moorea (~30 min), check into your overwater bungalow.

02

Lagoon day

Snorkeling right off your bungalow, lagoon tour.

Recommended: Miti Tours lagoon snorkeling ↗
03

Reef excursion

Swim with stingrays and reef sharks (shallow, guided, very safe), or a private lagoon picnic.

Recommended: Moorea Mahana Tours (stingrays + sharks) ↗
04

Interior of Moorea

Belvedere lookout, pineapple plantation tour, or a 4x4 safari through the mountains.

Recommended: Albert Tours 4x4 safari ↗
05

Free day

Pure relaxation, spa treatment.

06

Second free day / Tahiti

Optional day trip to Tahiti for shopping and a nice dinner.

07

Depart Papeete

Fly home.
