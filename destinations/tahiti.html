🌺 Papeete / Moorea

The classic honeymoon fantasy — lagoons, overwater bungalows, total quiet. Tahiti/Papeete is mostly a gateway; most of your time will be on Moorea for the postcard experience.

Season

Shoulder: September–October (dry season winding down, fewer visitors). Peak: July–August (dry season peak, most crowded/priciest for French Polynesia overall).

Tight

Itinerary
01

Arrive Papeete

Quick ferry to Moorea (~30 min), check into your overwater bungalow.

02

Lagoon day

Snorkeling right off your bungalow, lagoon tour.

03

Reef excursion

Swim with stingrays and reef sharks (shallow, guided, very safe), or a private lagoon picnic.

04

Interior of Moorea

Belvedere lookout, pineapple plantation tour, or a 4x4 safari through the mountains.

05

Free day

Pure relaxation, spa treatment.

06

Second free day / Tahiti

Optional day trip to Tahiti for shopping and a nice dinner.

07

Depart Papeete

Fly home.
