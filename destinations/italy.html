🇮🇹 Rome + Amalfi Coast

Elegant and unhurried — ruins and long dinners in Rome, cliffside relaxation and boat days on the coast. Classic-romantic, not adrenaline. 8 days on the ground, about 9 door-to-door.

Season

Shoulder: late September–mid October (warm, thinner crowds). Peak: July–August (hot, packed, Ferragosto crowds mid-August). Alaska's Rome route is seasonal, running through October 23, so this window lines up with the nonstop flight.

Comfortable

Comfortable with Poseidon-tier lodging in Positano; tight-to-over-budget with Le Sirenuse or Il San Pietro once Capri, transfers, and dinners are added.

Itinerary
01

Arrive Rome

Land early afternoon, check into your Airbnb (Monti or Trastevere), easy evening walk and dinner nearby. No sightseeing pressure.

02

Ancient Rome

Colosseum, Roman Forum, Palatine Hill in the morning; Pantheon and Trevi Fountain in the afternoon; long dinner in Trastevere.

Official tickets: Colosseum + Forum + Palatine ↗
03

Vatican + travel day

Vatican Museums and St. Peter's Basilica in the morning, then afternoon train to Naples (~1hr) and private transfer or ferry to Positano (~1–1.5hr).

Official tickets: Vatican Museums ↗
04

Positano

Beach day at Spiaggia Grande or a beach club, explore the boutiques and lemon groves.

Recommended beach club: Arienzo Beach Club ↗
05

Capri

Full-day boat excursion — Blue Grotto, Faraglioni rocks, lunch on the water.

Recommended boat tour: Capri Whales ↗
06

Ravello or Path of the Gods

Either the scenic hike (moderate difficulty, stunning views) or a mellower day trip to Ravello's cliffside gardens.

Trail info: Path of the Gods ↗ Villa Rufolo, Ravello ↗
07

Free day

Spa, beach, or slow mornings with a view. Farewell dinner.

08

Depart

Transfer back to Naples or Rome, fly home.
