🌿 Liberia / Guanacaste

Rainforest-meets-beach, wildlife-forward — sloths, monkeys, volcano views, zip-lining, all a short drive from your resort. More “nature adventure with beach downtime” than pure relaxation.

Season

July–October is Costa Rica's green/rainy season — genuinely the toughest destination on this list to time well. October is actually the wettest month in Guanacaste. If Costa Rica makes the shortlist, lean toward July rather than September/October — some years bring a brief mid-summer dry spell locally called the “veranillo,” though this isn't guaranteed. Mornings tend to be clear with afternoon downpours rather than all-day rain.
