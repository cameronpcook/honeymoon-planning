🇫🇯 Nadi / Mamanuca Islands

Warm, welcoming culture ("Fiji time"), a mix of overwater and beach resorts depending on island, generally more relaxed and less exclusive-feeling than French Polynesia — still stunning, more approachable price point. 7 days on the ground, about 8 door-to-door.

Season

Shoulder: April–June, September–October.

Peak: July–August (dry season, most popular).

Fiji Airways flies LAX–Nadi nonstop daily, so — unlike Rarotonga — this window isn't dictated by a weekly flight.

Budget for $7,500
$$$$$

Six nights at Likuliku (Fiji's only overwater bures, meals mostly included) runs lodging to about $5,700, but with meals bundled the extras are light — total lands around $6,500–7,300 depending on excursions. Genuinely more comfortable than Bora Bora at a similar experience level, as the original read suggested.
