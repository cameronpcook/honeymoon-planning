🌵 Cabo San Lucas

Desert-meets-ocean glamour — dramatic cliffs, upscale resorts, marina nightlife, easy day trips to quieter beach towns. Feels more like a polished resort escape than an immersive cultural trip.

Season

Your July–October window overlaps both sides: July–September is technically Baja's hurricane season (low but non-zero risk, hot and humid); October shifts into a much drier, more comfortable, lower-risk stretch. Push this one toward mid-to-late October specifically — September is the height of hurricane season and the most humid month, and the risk drops drastically by late October.
