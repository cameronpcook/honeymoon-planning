🇮🇸 Reykjavik

Adventure-forward and dramatic — waterfalls, glaciers, hot springs, black sand beaches. Less “romantic dinner,” more “in awe of the landscape together.” A good pick if you want your honeymoon to feel like an expedition.

Season

Alaska's route is summer-seasonal, so the realistic window is July–early September. July–August is peak (midnight sun, warmest, busiest, priciest). Early September is a soft shoulder — cooler, first shot at northern lights, fewer people.

Itinerary
01

Arrive Reykjavik

Settle in, easy walk downtown, dinner.

02

Golden Circle

Þingvellir National Park, Geysir geothermal area, Gullfoss waterfall.

03

South Coast

Seljalandsfoss and Skógafoss waterfalls, black sand beach at Reynisfjara, glacier views.

04

Glacier extension

Glacier hike or ice cave (seasonal) plus Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon and diamond beach if time allows.

05

Blue Lagoon or Sky Lagoon

Geothermal soak, spa day, easy pace after days of driving.

06

Snæfellsnes Peninsula or city day

Either “Iceland in miniature” scenery, or museums, shopping, and restaurants back in the capital.

07

Free day, Reykjavik

Whale watching, more city time, or a final soak.

08

Fly home.
