🇮🇸 Reykjavik

Adventure-forward and dramatic — waterfalls, glaciers, hot springs, black sand beaches. Less “romantic dinner,” more “in awe of the landscape together.” A good pick if you want your honeymoon to feel like an expedition.

Season

Peak: July–August (midnight sun, warmest, busiest, priciest).

Shoulder: Early September (cooler, first shot at northern lights, fewer people).

Alaska's Reykjavik route is summer-seasonal (late May–early September), so the realistic window is July–early September regardless.
