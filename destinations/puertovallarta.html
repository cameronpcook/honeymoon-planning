🌊 Puerto Vallarta

Warmer and more laid-back than Cabo — cobblestone streets, a real old-town feel, jungle backdrop meeting the beach. Less glossy-resort, more authentic-Mexico.

Season

Same hurricane-season logic as Cabo but on the Pacific mainland side — July–October carries rain/humidity risk, easing by October. Late October is specifically the most pleasant stretch as rainfall tapers off — lean toward the very end of your window here too.
