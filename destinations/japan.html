🇯🇵 Tokyo + Kyoto

A study in contrast — Tokyo is neon, food-obsessed, and energetic; Kyoto is temples, gardens, and quiet. Excellent for couples who want both stimulation and stillness in one trip.

Itinerary
01

Arrive Tokyo

Settle into your neighborhood, easy dinner nearby.

02

Shibuya/Shinjuku

Shibuya Crossing, Shinjuku Gyoen park, izakaya dinner.

03

Food + art

Tsukiji Outer Market breakfast, teamLab digital art museum, sushi omakase dinner.

04

Hakone day trip (optional)

Onsen, Mt. Fuji views, Hakone Open-Air Museum — or a slower Tokyo day instead.

05

Travel to Kyoto

Bullet train (~2.5hrs), check into a ryokan, evening kaiseki dinner.

06

Kyoto temples

Fushimi Inari's torii gates, Kiyomizu-dera, Gion district in the evening.

07

Arashiyama + free time

Bamboo grove, Tenryu-ji temple garden, wander at your own pace.

08

Depart

Via Tokyo or Osaka, depending on flight routing.
