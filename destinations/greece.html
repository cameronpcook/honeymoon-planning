🇹🇷🇬🇷 Istanbul + Santorini + Athens

Hagia Sophia's dome and the Grand Bazaar's stalls, then caldera views and sunset sailing in Santorini, then a last couple of days with the Acropolis and Plaka in Athens. Alaska's new Seattle–Athens nonstop is what put this on the list at all; the outbound into Istanbul rides Turkish Airlines business class on a Miles&Smiles award. 10 nights on the ground across three cities, 12 days door-to-door (Tue 9/21 – Sat 10/2, 2027).

Season

These dates: Tue Sep 21 – Sat Oct 2, 2027 — warm, thinner crowds, and locked in by Alaska's new seasonal route on the way home.

Peak: July–August (Aegean high season — hottest, most crowded, priciest; also hotter and more humid in Istanbul).

The Istanbul→Santorini leg is the one piece that isn't locked down — direct seasonal service exists but isn't confirmed for this exact date. See Flight Info.

Budget for $7,500
$$$$$

4 nights at Hotel Amira in the Old City (~$180/night) + 4 nights at Aqua Luxury Suites in Imerovigli (~$450/night) + 2 nights at AthensWas (~$375/night) runs lodging to about $3,150; add food, the two inter-city flights, the Bosphorus cruise, and the caldera catamaran, and the total lands around $6,200. Trading up to Four Seasons Sultanahmet, Canaves Oia, and Hotel Grande Bretagne is a genuinely different trip — that combination alone clears $8,500 in lodging before anything else.

Itinerary
01

Tue 9/21 — Depart Seattle

Turkish Airlines TK204, SEA → IST, business class on a Miles&Smiles award. Overnight in the air.

02

Wed 9/22 — Arrive Istanbul

Land in the afternoon, settle in, easy dinner near the hotel to shake off the flight.

03

Thu 9/23 — Old City

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Basilica Cistern, and Topkapı Palace — all within walking distance of each other in Sultanahmet.

04

Fri 9/24 — Bazaars + Bosphorus

Grand Bazaar and the Spice Market by day, a sunset Bosphorus cruise from Eminönü in the evening.

05

Sat 9/25 — Karaköy + the Asian side

Wander Karaköy, up the Galata Tower, then the ferry across to Kadıköy on the Asian side. Finish with a hammam.

06

Sun 9/26 — Fly to Santorini

IST → JTR, unconfirmed schedule (see Flight Info). Settle into the caldera-view hotel in Imerovigli or Oia.

07

Mon 9/27 — Caldera catamaran cruise

Swim stops at the volcanic hot springs, sail past the red and white beaches, sunset on the water.

Recommended: Caldera Yachting cruise ↗
08

Tue 9/28 — Fira–Oia hike + winery tour

The caldera-rim trail from Fira to Oia, then Assyrtiko tastings with caldera views.

Official site: Santo Wines ↗
09

Wed 9/29 — Slow day

Pool time, Perissa or Kamari's beaches, no plans. Sunset dinner to close out Santorini.

10

Thu 9/30 — Fly or ferry to Athens

Morning transfer to Athens (mode TBD). Afternoon in Plaka and Anafiotika, rooftop dinner with an Acropolis view.

11

The Acropolis right at opening, before the heat and crowds build, then the Acropolis Museum. Afternoon in Monastiraki and Koukaki.
